A small station with a big ear

Gagantara Radio began as a single microphone and a dream to put local voices on the air. Founder Subash Bhike built a station where farmers, students and shopkeepers could hear their own stories — voice beyond boundaries.

Today we broadcast 24 hours a day to listeners across Solapur and nearby towns, mixing Marathi, Hindi and English shows with community news, music and long-form interviews.

Everything we air is guided by one idea: local voices come first.
